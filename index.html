CULINARY DECORUM

Dining & Table Etiquette

  • ✓ Outside-In Rule: Work through your cutlery starting with outermost forks and knives first.
  • ✓ Napkin Placement: Place on your lap immediately upon sitting. If leaving briefly, leave it on your chair.
  • ✕ Phones Off The Table: Never place mobile devices or keys next to bread plates.
VIRTUAL COLLABORATION

Digital & Zoom Etiquette

  • ✓ Default Mute: Stay muted unless speaking to eliminate background noise.
  • ✓ Camera Framing: Keep camera at eye-level with natural front-facing light.
  • ✕ Multi-Tasking: Avoid typing loudly or gazing at secondary monitors during discussions.
PROFESSIONAL INTEGRITY

Corporate & Email Poise

  • ✓ Email Response Windows: Acknowledge external emails within 24 hours.
  • ✓ Punctuality Protocol: Arriving 3 minutes early is on time; arriving on time is late.
  • ✕ Unwarranted 'Reply All': Reserve mass replies strictly for enterprise-critical broadcasts.
PUBLIC DOMAIN

Public & Shared Spaces

  • ✓ Headphones Mandatory: Never play speakerphone calls or reels in transit or lounges.
  • ✓ Step Aside First: Allow departing passengers out of elevators and trains before entering.
  • ✕ Personal Space: Respect the 2-foot bubble in queues and checkouts.
SOCIAL DYNAMICS

Conversational Mastery

  • ✓ Active Listening: Wait 1 full second after someone finishes before responding.
  • ✓ The 3-Minute Rule: Never monopolize group chats or conversations with monologues.
  • ✕ Unsolicited Critiques: Never comment on someone’s weight, appearance, or meal choices.
HOST & GUEST CODE

RSVP & Host Etiquette

  • ✓ RSVP Timeliness: Confirm attendance within 48 hours of invitation receipt.
  • ✓ Host Gifts: Never arrive empty-handed (chocolates, specialty teas, or flowers).
  • ✕ Surprise Plus-Ones: Never bring unannounced guests to private reservations.
